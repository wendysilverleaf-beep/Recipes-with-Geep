Gold Standard Chocolate Ice Cream

The family’s favourite exceptionally rich, silky chocolate ice cream. Exact ingredient proportions of the successfully tested V2, scaled to 60% for a smaller Cuisinart batch. The original V2 scooped beautifully straight from the freezer; the resized yield is not yet tested.

Yield: Approximately 900 ml (estimated)

Prep 20 min · Cook 15 min · Chill 6–12 hours · Freeze overnight

Ingredients

  • 270 g full-cream milk
  • 288 g fresh cream
  • 84 g caster sugar
  • 33 g dextrose
  • 6 g golden syrup
  • 24 g whole milk powder
  • 30 g dark cocoa powder
  • 24 g milk chocolate, chopped
  • 0.6 g guar gum
  • 1.2 g vanilla extract

Thermomix TM6 method

  1. Weigh the caster sugar, dextrose and guar gum into a dry bowl and mix thoroughly. Stir in the whole milk powder and cocoa powder to distribute the guar evenly.
  2. Add the milk, cream, dry ingredient mixture and golden syrup to the Thermomix bowl. Mix 20 sec / speed 4, scraping down if necessary.
  3. Cook 12–15 min / 85°C / speed 3, until the mixture reaches 85°C and is smooth. Avoid prolonged holding at temperature.
  4. Add the chopped milk chocolate and vanilla extract. Allow the chocolate to melt for 1 minute, then blend 20–30 sec / speed 5 until silky smooth.
  5. Transfer to a clean container and cool rapidly in an ice bath. Refrigerate covered for 6–12 hours, preferably overnight, at 4°C or below.
  6. Stir the chilled base, then churn in the Cuisinart Freeze Ease Pro until thick and softly frozen, approximately 25–35 minutes. Stop if the motor labours; do not overfill.
  7. Scrape into a 1-litre freezer container, level and cover. Freeze until firm, preferably overnight. Test scooping directly from the freezer.

Recipe notes

Tasting record (full-sized V2): “The most sublime thing.” Beautifully scoopable straight from the freezer, exceptionally smooth mouthfeel and rich chocolate flavour. Best chocolate ice cream the family has tasted.

Scaling: All ingredients are 60% of the tested V2. The V2 yielded about 1.5 litres and was too large for comfortable churning. This version has about 760 g of base and is expected to yield around 900 ml, but actual overrun and finished volume need confirming. If it approaches the machine’s safe fill limit, churn less at once.

Important: Use whole milk powder, not skim milk powder. Do not change the sugar or stabiliser balance without testing. This recipe is a scaled version, not yet separately taste-tested.
