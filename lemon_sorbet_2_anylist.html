🍋 Traditional Lemon Sorbet 2.0

A bright, intensely lemony family favourite, gently refined for a smoother texture.

Yield: approximately 750 ml  ·  Thermomix: 5 min  ·  Chill: 4 hours or overnight  ·  Churn: 25–35 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g freshly squeezed lemon juice
  • 300 g water
  • 155 g caster sugar
  • 45 g homemade invert sugar
  • 0.15 g xanthan gum
  • Finely grated zest of 2 lemons
  • Tiny pinch of salt

Thermomix method

  1. Thoroughly mix the xanthan gum into the dry caster sugar to distribute it evenly.
  2. Place the water, caster sugar and xanthan mixture, homemade invert sugar and lemon zest in the Thermomix bowl. Heat 5 min / 80°C / speed 2.
  3. Allow the syrup to cool for approximately 15–20 minutes.
  4. Add the freshly squeezed lemon juice and salt. Mix 10 sec / speed 3. Do not cook the lemon juice.
  5. Strain through a fine sieve to remove the lemon zest.
  6. Refrigerate until thoroughly chilled, at least 4 hours or preferably overnight.
  7. Optionally measure the Brix of the chilled base and record it for comparison. Churn in the Cuisinart compressor ice cream maker for approximately 25–35 minutes, until thick and snowy.
  8. Transfer to a freezer-safe container and freeze until firm. Assess scoopability after 24 hours in the freezer.
Testing note: This is a gentle variation on the original family-favourite recipe. Aim to preserve its sharp, fresh lemon flavour while comparing smoothness and scoopability. A Brix reading around 27–30° is a rough reference, not a target to force with extra sugar.
