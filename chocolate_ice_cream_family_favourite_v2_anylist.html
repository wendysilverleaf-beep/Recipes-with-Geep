Chocolate Ice Cream – Family Favourite v2

Prep 20 min · Cook 15 min · Chill 6–12 h · Freeze overnight

Rich chocolate ice cream with dextrose for improved scoopability.

Yield: About 1.27 kg base

Ingredients

  • 450 g full-cream milk
  • 480 g fresh cream (500 ml)
  • 140 g caster sugar
  • 55 g dextrose
  • 10 g golden syrup
  • 40 g whole milk powder
  • 50 g dark cocoa powder
  • 40 g milk chocolate, chopped
  • 1.0 g guar gum
  • 2 g vanilla extract

Preparation Steps

  1. Mix the caster sugar, dextrose and guar gum thoroughly in a dry bowl so the gum disperses evenly. Whisk in the whole milk powder and cocoa powder.
  2. Place the milk and cream in a saucepan. Warm gently, whisking in the dry ingredients gradually until smooth.
  3. Add the golden syrup. Heat while stirring to about 82–85°C, avoiding scorching; hold briefly, then remove from the heat.
  4. Add the chopped milk chocolate and vanilla. Blend carefully with a stick blender until completely smooth.
  5. Cool the mixture rapidly in an ice bath, then refrigerate at 4°C or below for 6–12 hours.
  6. Stir the cold base, pour into the Cuisinart compressor machine without exceeding its fill limit, and churn until thick and softly frozen (typically 25–35 minutes).
  7. Transfer promptly to a shallow freezer-safe container, level the surface, cover and freeze until firm, ideally overnight. Test scoopability directly from the freezer and again after 5 and 10 minutes.

Notes

Experimental update of the family-favourite chocolate ice cream. Uses 500 ml (480 g) cream and whole milk powder instead of skim milk powder. Dextrose raised to 55 g to improve scoopability. Record freezer temperature, texture and melt before making further changes. Yield depends on overrun.
