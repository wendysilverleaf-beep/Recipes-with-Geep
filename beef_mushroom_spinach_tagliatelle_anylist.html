Beef, Mushroom & Spinach Tagliatelle

Serves 3 · Prep 15 minutes · Cook 25 minutes

A savoury, creamy family favourite.

Ingredients

  • 250 g beef, thinly sliced
  • 225 g tagliatelle
  • 250 g button mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 1 small onion or leek, chopped
  • 2 handfuls spinach
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 125 ml beef stock
  • 2 tbsp crème fraîche
  • 40 g Parmesan, grated, plus extra to serve
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 15 g butter
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Zest of ½ lemon
  • Fresh parsley, chopped (optional)

Method

  1. Cook the tagliatelle in salted water until al dente. Reserve ½ cup pasta cooking water, then drain.
  2. Season the beef with salt and pepper. Heat the olive oil in a large pan and sear the beef in two batches for 1–2 minutes per batch. Remove and set aside.
  3. Add the butter and sauté the mushrooms for 6–8 minutes until browned. Add the onion or leek and cook until softened, then add the garlic and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Stir in the Dijon mustard and Worcestershire sauce. Add the beef stock and simmer for about 3 minutes to reduce slightly.
  5. Stir in the crème fraîche and Parmesan, adding a splash of reserved pasta water to make a silky sauce.
  6. Return the beef to the pan and add the spinach, stirring just until wilted. Fold in the tagliatelle, adding more pasta water if necessary.
  7. Finish with lemon zest, black pepper and extra Parmesan. Garnish with parsley if using.
